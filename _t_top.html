About

Pharmacology researcher with 2.5 years of graduate laboratory experience in anticancer drug discovery, target identification, and mechanism-of-action studies, complemented by 2 years of undergraduate research in heterogeneous catalysis and chemical engineering.

Graduate research focused on developing novel anticancer agents for radioresistant breast cancer, including the identification and characterization of small-molecule compounds targeting long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase 4 (ACSL4). Proficient in DARTS, IP/Co-IP, siRNA knockdown, Western blotting, RT-PCR, and immunofluorescence, backed by an MSc in Pharmacology.

Seeking a PhD position in molecular oncology or cancer pharmacology focused on drug response and resistance, target validation, and the preclinical evaluation of novel therapeutic strategies.

Research Experience

Graduate Researcher at Dongguk University

Sep 2023 – Feb 2026

Laboratory of Pharmacology, College of Medicine · Gyeongju, South Korea

  • Screened candidate compounds for anticancer activity using MTT, SRB, and colony formation assays.
  • Investigated durumamide D, a novel microbial metabolite, as a potential ACSL4 inhibitor in radioresistant breast cancer, forming the basis of the MSc thesis.
  • Examined direct binding between durumamide D and ACSL4 using DARTS and characterized protein–protein interactions by IP/Co-IP, supporting the identification and validation of ACSL4 as a molecular target.
  • Validated target dependence and characterized the mechanism of action through siRNA-mediated ACSL4 knockdown and cell cycle analysis.
  • Analyzed gene and protein expression using RT-PCR and Western blotting, and investigated protein subcellular localization by immunofluorescence.
  • Contributed to 3 manuscripts: 1 published, 1 under revision, and 1 in preparation as first author.

Undergraduate Research Assistant at VNU University of Science

Sep 2020 – Jun 2022

Laboratory of Petroleum Chemistry · Hanoi, Vietnam

  • Developed heterogeneous catalysts containing transition-metal nanoparticles supported on microporous and mesoporous materials for the conversion of organic compounds.
  • Synthesized a CuTiO3 catalyst and applied it to isoeugenol oxidation, forming the basis of the BSc thesis.
  • Characterized materials and reaction products with 5 analytical techniques (GC-MS, FT-IR, UV-Vis, XRD, and SEM).
  • Presented findings at the Faculty of Chemistry Scientific Conference (April 2021) and helped prepare research reports and manuscripts.
